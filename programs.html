Programs & Events

Year-round CPD activities designed to elevate customer management expertise. Structured learning pathways, networking opportunities, and professional development through expert-led programs.

Continuous Learning Throughout the Year

Our comprehensive annual program calendar ensures you're always developing new skills, staying current with industry trends, and connecting with peers across Southern Africa.

From monthly virtual tutorials to our flagship Customer Service Week Connect event, every program is designed to build practical capabilities that directly impact your customer experience outcomes.

The Connect Voice

Monthly | 2 Hours | Virtual/In-person

Why Attend Our Programs?

Each program is carefully designed to deliver measurable value for customer management professionals. You'll gain practical skills, industry insights, recognized certifications, and connections that accelerate your career.

All programs include: Expert facilitators, practical case studies, networking opportunities, digital resources, CPD points, and post-event support materials.
